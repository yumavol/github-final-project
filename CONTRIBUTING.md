# Contributing

## Our Pledge

I want this to be a friendly place for anyone who stops by. It doesn't matter where you're from, what you look like, what you believe, or how much coding you've done. All contributions, bug reports, bug fixes, documentation improvements, enhancements, and ideas are welcome. If you're here to learn or help out, you're in the right place.

## Our Standards

Be nice. Disagreeing is fine, but keep it about the code, not the person. Feedback is great, just say it like you'd want to hear it. No insults, no harassment, no creepy or sexual stuff, and don't post anyone's personal info.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

I'm the one keeping an eye on things here. If a comment, issue, or PR crosses the line, I'll edit it, close it, or delete it. Most of the time I'll tell you why.

## Scope

This covers everything connected to the project: issues, PRs, discussions, and anywhere else people talk about it. If you're speaking for the project somewhere else, the same rules apply there too.

## Enforcement

If someone is making you uncomfortable, message me at my linkedin profile. I'll read every report and take it seriously, and I won't share who sent it.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Small stuff usually gets a private heads-up. If it keeps happening, you might get blocked for a while. Anything really bad, or the same problem again and again, means you're out for good.
